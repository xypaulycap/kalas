Health, Safety, Environment & Quality (HSEQ)

Our commitment to excellence is anchored in rigorous HSEQ governance. We prioritize the well-being of our workforce, the safety of our terminals, environmental stewardship, and uncompromised service quality.

ACCREDITED COMPLIANCE
ISO 9001 · ISO 14001 · ISO 45001
FOUR PILLARS OF HSEQ GOVERNANCE

Zero-Compromise Operational Standards

Every tank transfer, pipeline weld, and vessel berthing operation is governed by our four core HSEQ pillars.

Health

Promoting physical and mental well-being for all employees and contractors through comprehensive occupational health programs, ergonomic workspaces, and proactive medical monitoring.

Safety

Maintaining a zero-accident culture through strict adherence to safety protocols, continuous hazard identification, permit-to-work enforcement, and emergency preparedness.

Environment

Minimizing our ecological footprint through sustainable practices, vapor recovery units, spill-prevention engineering, and strict compliance with environmental regulations.

Quality

Delivering reliable, high-standard storage and shipping services that consistently meet or exceed client specifications, international codes, and custody-transfer benchmarks.

INTERNATIONAL ACCREDITATIONS

Certified Management Systems

Independently audited and certified across our corporate headquarters and international port terminals.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

ISO 9001

Quality Management System

Demonstrates our ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements across tank storage and maritime transport.

ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP

ISO 14001

Environmental Management

ACTIVE TERMINAL PROGRAMS

Environmental Initiatives & Safety Programs in Action

Environmental Initiatives

ECO-COMPLIANCE

We actively invest in clean-terminal infrastructure to reduce emissions, eliminate waste, and protect marine and coastal ecosystems surrounding our port facilities:

  • Carbon footprint reduction strategies: Deployment of closed-loop vapor recovery units (VRUs), internal floating roofs, and electrified terminal pumping manifolds.
  • Waste recycling and management programs: Certified hydrocarbon sludge recovery, closed-circuit tank washwater treatment, and hazardous waste segregation.
  • Energy-efficient terminal lighting: Full conversion of tank farm perimeters and marine jetties to ATEX-rated low-consumption LED illumination systems.

Safety Programs

ZERO-INCIDENT MANDATE

Our operational safety culture empowers every engineer, terminal operator, and vessel crew member with proactive training and real-time risk mitigation protocols:

  • Regular emergency response drills: Scheduled multi-agency fire suppression, marine spill containment, and terminal evacuation simulations across all hubs.
  • Behavior-based safety observations: Continuous peer-to-peer safety auditing, stop-work authority enforcement, and proactive near-miss reporting.
  • Comprehensive risk assessments: Rigorous HAZOP, LOPA, and job safety analyses (JSA) conducted prior to every maintenance turnaround or cargo transfer.
