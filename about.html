WHO WE ARE

Leading Provider of Oil Tank Storage Solutions

At Katlashipping B.V., we specialize in delivering world-class oil tank storage and maritime logistics solutions tailored to meet the complex demands of the global energy industry.

With years of operational experience and a steadfast commitment to safety, quality, and sustainability, we ensure the secure and efficient handling of liquid commodities. From custom-built storage tanks and turnkey tank farm construction to comprehensive maintenance and pipeline installation services, our expert team is dedicated to supporting your operations every step of the way.

Operating across strategic maritime hubs in Rotterdam, Houston, Singapore, and the United Arab Emirates, Katlashipping B.V. bridges onshore containment engineering with global bulk liquid and project cargo shipping.
