2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by corporate website operators to make their platforms function efficiently, remember user preferences (such as multi-language selection and cookie consent acknowledgment), and provide reporting information.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary & Functional Storage: Essential for core navigation, form security, and remembering your cookie consent status (`katla_cookies_accepted`).
  • Language & Localization Preferences: Used by our integrated translation module (`googtrans`) to display terminal and engineering documentation in your selected language (English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Swedish, or Chinese).
  • Live Support & Communications: Our interactive customer support widget (Smartsupp) uses session storage to maintain continuity during live commercial or technical inquiries.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies at any time. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though certain interactive features such as persistent language translation may require re-selection.
